# Incident Response Readiness

- [ ] Severity criteria reflect service impact.
- [ ] Human incident lead and responder roles are defined.
- [ ] Escalation and communication routes are accessible.
- [ ] Detection signals and runbooks are available.
- [ ] Evidence preservation and sensitive-data controls are defined.
- [ ] Recovery validation criteria are documented.
- [ ] Post-incident review and action tracking are defined.
- [ ] A safe exercise or review has validated the process.
