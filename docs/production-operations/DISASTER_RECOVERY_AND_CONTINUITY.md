# Disaster Recovery and Business Continuity

## Scope
Identify scenarios that could make a service or critical dependency unavailable: infrastructure loss, region failure, data corruption, cyberattack, provider outage, credential compromise or loss of key personnel.

## Objectives
RTO is the target time to restore service. RPO is the target maximum data-loss interval. Obtain these targets from accountable business owners; do not invent them. If targets are unknown, record the gap and its consequence.

## Strategy
Select recovery methods proportionate to criticality and cost: restore from backup, rebuild infrastructure, fail over, use alternate providers or operate a documented manual process. Identify dependencies and single points of failure.

## Plans and exercises
Document activation authority, decision criteria, communications, recovery sequence, validation, fallback and return-to-normal steps. Exercise plans safely and record actual outcomes, deviations and actions.

## Claims
Do not claim high availability, disaster tolerance or tested failover based only on architecture diagrams or provider features. Require executed evidence.
