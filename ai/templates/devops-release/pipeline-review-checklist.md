# Pipeline Review Checklist

- [ ] Pipeline source is version-controlled and reviewed.
- [ ] Branch and environment protections are enabled.
- [ ] Permissions are least-privilege and scoped.
- [ ] Third-party actions/plugins/images are pinned and reviewed.
- [ ] Untrusted PR workflows cannot access production secrets.
- [ ] Dependency lockfiles and integrity checks are used.
- [ ] Tests, static analysis, security scans, and policy gates are explicit.
- [ ] Failure suppression and broad retries are absent or justified.
- [ ] Logs do not disclose secrets or sensitive data.
- [ ] Artifacts are immutable and traceable.
- [ ] Runner isolation, cleanup, and network controls are appropriate.
- [ ] Human approval protects production and high-impact operations.
- [ ] Evidence and ownership are recorded.
