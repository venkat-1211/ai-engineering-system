# Prompt 03 — Compliance Applicability Review

Prepare a preliminary applicability and evidence review. This is not a legal opinion.

## Instructions
1. Establish jurisdiction, entity, industry, data, processing purpose, users, contracts, and system function from verified context.
2. Identify potentially relevant obligations only when there is a reasonable basis.
3. Record source name, version/date, jurisdiction, and authoritative reference when available.
4. Separate `Applicable`, `Not applicable`, `Potentially applicable`, and `Not assessed`.
5. Explain assumptions and facts still required.
6. Map verified applicable requirements to controls, owners, and evidence.
7. Escalate legal interpretation to qualified counsel or compliance personnel.

Never claim compliance or certification from a checklist, planned control, or AI-generated assertion. Do not modify files or expose sensitive evidence.
