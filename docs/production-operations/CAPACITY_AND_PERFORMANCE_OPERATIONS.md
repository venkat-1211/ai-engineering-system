# Capacity and Performance Operations

## Operational capacity
Track relevant resource and service limits: CPU, memory, storage, connections, queue depth, throughput, rate limits, network and third-party quotas.

## Baselines and forecasts
Use measured workload patterns and growth assumptions. Identify source, period, confidence and limitations. Do not present estimates as observed facts.

## Saturation signals
Set thresholds based on service behavior and recovery lead time. Correlate resource pressure with latency, errors, queueing and user impact.

## Scaling
Document scaling triggers, limits, costs, cooldowns and failure modes. Test scaling behavior where feasible. Avoid unbounded autoscaling or automatic expensive changes without approved controls.

## Reviews
Review capacity after major releases, material traffic changes and recurring incidents. Maintain a prioritized plan for bottlenecks and capacity risks.
