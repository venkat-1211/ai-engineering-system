# Prompt 05 — Audit Readiness Review

Perform a read-only audit-readiness assessment for the stated scope.

## Verify
- requirements map to controls;
- control owners are identified;
- evidence references exist and are accessible to authorized reviewers;
- evidence scope, date, provenance, and verification method are clear;
- approvals and exceptions are traceable;
- known gaps and exclusions are disclosed;
- secrets and unnecessary personal data are not exposed.

## Rules
Do not create or alter evidence. Do not infer that a control operated because a policy exists. Do not claim certification, audit success, or compliance. Do not access restricted evidence without authorization.

## Output
Classify each item as `Verified`, `Partially verified`, `Not verified`, `Not applicable`, or `Blocked`, with evidence references and limitations. End with a scoped readiness conclusion, not a blanket compliance claim.
