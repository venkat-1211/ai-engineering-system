# Governance Readiness Checklist

- [ ] Project purpose, scope, users, and operating context are documented.
- [ ] Accountable human owner is identified.
- [ ] Decision authority and escalation path are confirmed.
- [ ] Relevant technical, security, data, quality, and release roles are assigned or explicitly not applicable.
- [ ] Risk assessment depth is proportionate to impact.
- [ ] Compliance applicability is assessed or explicitly marked not assessed.
- [ ] Evidence sources and access controls are defined.
- [ ] Quality measures have definitions, sources, and owners.
- [ ] Exception process and expiry controls are understood.
- [ ] Review triggers and cadence are documented.
- [ ] Open gaps and assumptions are disclosed.
- [ ] Required approvals are recorded, not inferred.
