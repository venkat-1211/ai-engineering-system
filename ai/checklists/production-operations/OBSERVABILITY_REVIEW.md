# Observability Review

- [ ] Logs are structured, useful and appropriately redacted.
- [ ] Metrics have clear units, sources and bounded labels.
- [ ] Trace/correlation context works across relevant boundaries.
- [ ] Dashboards answer operational questions.
- [ ] Alerts are actionable, owned and routed.
- [ ] Runbooks are linked from alerts.
- [ ] Retention and access controls are appropriate.
- [ ] End-to-end telemetry and notification paths were tested.
- [ ] Gaps and evidence are recorded.
