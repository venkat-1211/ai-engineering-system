# Risk Assessment Checklist

- [ ] Intended use, users, affected stakeholders, and context are understood.
- [ ] Assets, data, dependencies, and trust boundaries are identified.
- [ ] Threats, failure modes, and misuse scenarios are considered.
- [ ] AI-specific risks are considered where AI is actually used.
- [ ] Likelihood and impact scales are defined.
- [ ] Existing controls have supporting evidence.
- [ ] Residual risk and uncertainty are recorded.
- [ ] Treatment decision has an accountable owner.
- [ ] Risk acceptance, if any, is approved by an authorized human.
- [ ] Stop conditions and escalation paths are clear.
- [ ] Reassessment triggers are documented.
