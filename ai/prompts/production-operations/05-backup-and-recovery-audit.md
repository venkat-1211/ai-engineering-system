# Prompt 05 — Backup and Recovery Audit

Read root instructions and Stage 7 backup/disaster-recovery standards. Assess documented backup scope, retention, access separation, encryption, restore procedures, recovery objectives and exercise evidence.

Do not trigger backups, restore data, fail over infrastructure, access production systems or run destructive commands. Do not expose credentials, keys, personal data or confidential infrastructure details.

Clearly distinguish configured, executed, tested and independently verified controls. A backup job success is not proof of restoration. Report unknown RTO/RPO as unknown, identify evidence gaps and recommend a safe, human-approved test plan.
