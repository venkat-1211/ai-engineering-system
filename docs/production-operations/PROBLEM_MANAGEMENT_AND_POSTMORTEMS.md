# Problem Management and Postmortems

## Purpose
Incident response restores service; problem management reduces recurrence and systemic risk.

## Analysis
Build a factual timeline from available evidence. Distinguish confirmed facts, hypotheses and unknowns. Examine contributing technical, process, dependency and organizational factors. Avoid blame and unsupported single-cause conclusions.

## Corrective actions
Each action needs an owner, priority, due date, expected outcome and verification method. Prioritize systemic prevention, detection and mitigation over superficial documentation-only actions.

## Postmortem
For significant incidents, document impact, detection, response, recovery, what worked, what did not, contributing factors, evidence gaps and tracked actions. Apply severity-based proportionality.

## Effectiveness
Verify completed actions reduce the relevant risk or improve the measured outcome. Reopen or revise actions when evidence shows they were ineffective.
