# Compliance Evidence Review Checklist

- [ ] Jurisdiction, entity, industry, data, purpose, and system context are recorded.
- [ ] Potential obligations have authoritative source references and versions.
- [ ] Applicability decisions have rationale, owner, and date.
- [ ] Legal/compliance interpretation is referred to qualified authority where needed.
- [ ] Each applicable requirement maps to a control and owner.
- [ ] Evidence is authentic, relevant, scoped, and sufficiently current.
- [ ] Evidence limitations and untested areas are disclosed.
- [ ] Secrets and unnecessary personal data are excluded or protected.
- [ ] No unsupported compliance, certification, or audit-success claim is made.
