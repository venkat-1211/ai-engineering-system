# Prompt 03 — Knowledge Retrieval and Validation
Confirm project boundary and authorization. Search exact identifiers first, then broader concepts. Filter by project, component, status, version, date, sensitivity, and lifecycle. Inspect original sources for important claims. Relevance scores and repeated summaries are not proof. Report conflicts, stale/incomplete/inaccessible sources. Do not disclose unauthorized content or modify records.

Deliver ranked sources with references and trust labels; validation against current evidence; conflicts/limits; an answer distinguishing facts from inference; and missing evidence/next verification.
