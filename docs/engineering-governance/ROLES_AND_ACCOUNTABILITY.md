# Roles and Accountability

## Purpose

Make ownership explicit. Adapt role names to the real organization; do not invent people, job titles, or delegated authority.

## Suggested roles

- **Executive / sponsor:** provides direction and resources; accepts only risks within formally delegated authority.
- **Project owner:** accountable for project outcomes, scope, stakeholders, and governance completion.
- **Engineering owner:** accountable for technical decisions, implementation quality, and technical evidence.
- **Security owner:** advises on security risks and reviews security controls and exceptions.
- **Data owner:** determines permitted data use, access, retention, and disclosure.
- **Compliance or legal advisor:** interprets applicable obligations within their competence.
- **Quality owner:** defines quality measures and verifies quality reporting.
- **Release owner:** authorizes releases under the organization's release process.
- **Auditor / independent reviewer:** evaluates evidence independently of the activity being reviewed.
- **AI agent / assistant:** performs bounded delegated analysis or implementation; has no inherent approval authority.

## Assignment rules

1. Name one accountable owner for each material decision and action.
2. Distinguish accountable owner, responsible contributor, reviewer, and approver.
3. Record delegated authority and its limits.
4. Avoid self-approval for high-risk work where independent review is required.
5. If no qualified owner exists, mark the decision `Blocked` and escalate.
6. Keep ownership current when people or responsibilities change.

## RACI guidance

RACI may be used where useful:
- Responsible: performs the work.
- Accountable: answerable for the outcome; ideally one per decision.
- Consulted: provides input before the decision.
- Informed: receives the decision or status.

RACI is a coordination aid, not proof that authority was legally or organizationally delegated.
