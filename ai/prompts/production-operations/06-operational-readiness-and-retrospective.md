# Prompt 06 — Operational Readiness and Retrospective

Read root instructions and Stages 1–7. Evaluate the service against the production operational readiness checklist using actual evidence.

Review ownership, observability, alerting, runbooks, change/rollback, incident readiness, backup/restore, disaster recovery, capacity and operational security as applicable. Mark each item Passed, Failed, Not run, Blocked, Not applicable (with rationale), or Requires human approval. Do not infer success from documentation alone.

This is a read-only assessment. Do not modify files, change production systems, deploy, rotate secrets or run destructive tests. Never expose secrets or personal/confidential data. End with a readiness decision, residual risks, evidence gaps and prioritized actions. Human approval remains mandatory for production risk acceptance and operational changes.
