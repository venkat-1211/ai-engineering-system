# Prompt 05 — Evidence-Based Retrospective
Analyze supplied completed work, defect, incident, or delivery. Establish scope/timeline/impact; collect evidence; separate facts, interpretations, hypotheses, and opinions; avoid blame and unsupported root-cause claims. Do not generalize from one event without validation. Do not expose secrets or personal/customer data. Do not publish shared lessons.

Deliver event summary and sources; confirmed facts/unknowns/hypotheses; contributing factors and limitations; what worked/failed; candidate `ai/templates/knowledge-management/LESSON_LEARNED_RECORD.md`; actions/effectiveness measures; scope recommendation and required human review.
