# Knowledge Reuse and Standardization

Start with task-local or project-local scope. Before promotion: confirm owner and authorization; sanitize identifiers, secrets, personal/customer data, and confidential implementation details; validate general applicability; record versions, prerequisites, limitations, and counterexamples; obtain required human approval; retain safe provenance.

Promote a repeated practice to a standard only after evidence and domain-owner review. Re-check licensing, security, compatibility, tenancy, and business constraints before reuse. Agents can propose candidates but cannot publish shared standards or claim approval.
