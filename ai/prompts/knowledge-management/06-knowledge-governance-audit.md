# Prompt 06 — Knowledge Governance Audit (Read-Only)
Restrictions: do not create/edit/delete/rename/format files; install dependencies; change permissions; commit/push; access unauthorized systems; or expose secrets. Do not assume a database, vector store, or policy exists without evidence.

Audit inventory, taxonomy, scope/sensitivity, trust labels, provenance, ownership, freshness, retrieval authorization, cross-project isolation, lifecycle, decision approval, handoff safety, outcome metrics, internal links, and consistency with root instructions/earlier standards.

Report PASS / PASS WITH FINDINGS / FAIL / BLOCKED; exact scope/files inspected; severity, reference, issue, impact, recommendation; verified and unverifiable controls; and explicitly state no files changed. Do not claim legal/security certification.
