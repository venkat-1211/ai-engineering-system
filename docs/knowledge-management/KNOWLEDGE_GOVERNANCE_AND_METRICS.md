# Knowledge Governance and Metrics

Define who may create, own, review, approve, publish, access, and retire each knowledge class. Minimum controls: classification/access; provenance/trust; ownership/freshness; decision approval/history; project isolation/sanitization; exceptions/auditability; correction/removal process.

Exceptions need reason, scope, risk, compensating controls, owner, approver, expiry/review, and closure evidence. Agents may draft, not approve.

Measure outcomes: time to find correct knowledge; retrieval precision; stale-record remediation time; repeated defect recurrence; onboarding/handoff recovery time; evidence/ownership coverage for high-impact records; unauthorized retrieval/leakage; validated reuse outcomes. Define baselines before targets; do not invent metrics. Avoid rewarding document volume.
