# Prompt 04 — Incident and Problem Management Review

Read repository instructions and Stage 7 incident standards. Review supplied incident records and repository procedures in a read-only manner.

Assess detection, severity, ownership, escalation, communications, evidence handling, mitigation safety, recovery verification, postmortem quality and corrective-action effectiveness. Separate facts from hypotheses. Do not blame individuals or infer causes unsupported by evidence.

Do not modify files, run production commands, or expose secrets, personal data or confidential details. Return prioritized gaps and practical corrective actions with human owners to be assigned.
