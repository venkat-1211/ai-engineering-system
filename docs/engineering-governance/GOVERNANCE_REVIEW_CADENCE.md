# Governance Review Cadence

## Purpose

Set review frequency based on risk, change rate, and organizational requirements. The examples below are starting points, not universal mandates.

## Suggested review triggers

- project initiation or major scope change;
- new data category, processing purpose, or user group;
- architecture, deployment, or supplier change;
- new AI model, tool, capability, or permission;
- security incident or significant defect;
- material legal, regulatory, contractual, or policy update;
- risk or exception expiry;
- production release or major operational change;
- scheduled periodic review.

## Review agenda

1. Confirm scope, owners, and previous actions.
2. Review new and changed risks.
3. Review compliance applicability and evidence freshness.
4. Review exceptions, approvals, expiry, and remediation.
5. Review quality metrics and their limitations.
6. Review incidents, audit findings, and corrective actions.
7. Decide actions, owners, due dates, and escalation.
8. Record decisions and next review date.

## Cadence selection

Document why the chosen cadence is adequate for the system's risk and change profile. High-risk or rapidly changing systems may require more frequent review. Low-risk stable projects may use lighter review.

## Meeting records

Record attendees by role, date, scope, evidence reviewed, decisions, unresolved items, action owners, and next review. Do not imply attendance or approval that did not occur.
