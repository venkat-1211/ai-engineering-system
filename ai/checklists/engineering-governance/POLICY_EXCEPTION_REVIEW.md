# Policy Exception Review Checklist

- [ ] Exact policy requirement and affected scope are identified.
- [ ] Business/technical rationale and alternatives are documented.
- [ ] Risk and residual risk are assessed.
- [ ] Compensating controls are defined and verifiable.
- [ ] Accountable owner and remediation owner are assigned.
- [ ] Authorized human approver is identified.
- [ ] Approval and conditions are recorded.
- [ ] Expiry date and review cadence are set.
- [ ] Renewal requires fresh review and approval.
- [ ] Expired exceptions are not treated as active.
- [ ] Closure includes evidence or a formally approved retirement decision.
