# Prompt 01 — Production Operations Intake

Act as a production operations architect. Read root `AGENTS.md` and relevant Stages 1–6 before analysis. Inspect the actual repository and available evidence; do not assume a production deployment exists.

Identify application components, environments, deployment paths, data stores, external dependencies, owners, service criticality and known operational controls. Do not open or print secret-bearing files unless strictly necessary and authorized; never disclose secret values or personal/confidential data.

Separate observed facts, assumptions, unknowns and recommendations. Map applicable Stage 7 standards. Produce a concise service operations record and prioritized gaps. Do not modify files, access production systems, execute commands that change state, or claim controls work without evidence. Ask for human input where ownership, business impact or targets are unknown.
