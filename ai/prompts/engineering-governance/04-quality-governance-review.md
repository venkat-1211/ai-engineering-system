# Prompt 04 — Engineering Quality Governance Review

Review the quality measurement approach for a project or engineering portfolio.

## Inspect
- metric definitions and formulas;
- source data and collection method;
- baseline, period, target, and trend;
- ownership and review cadence;
- missing data and known limitations;
- incentives or gaming risks;
- balance across delivery, reliability, defects, security, and maintainability.

## Rules
Do not invent actual values, baselines, targets, or trends. Distinguish measured data from estimates and goals. Do not rank individuals using simplistic metrics. Do not modify files unless separately authorized by the user. Do not expose secrets or personal/confidential data in reports, examples, or evidence.

## Output
1. Metric inventory.
2. Definition and data-quality gaps.
3. Misleading or gameable measures.
4. Recommended balanced scorecard.
5. Decisions and owners required.
6. Evidence limitations.
