# Prompt 02 — Knowledge Capture
Capture candidate knowledge from supplied work/review/incident. Inspect actual evidence. Separate observed facts, user-provided statements, inference, hypotheses, and recommendations. Assign narrowest scope. Add references, versions, owner, status, and review trigger only when known; otherwise mark Unknown/Unassigned. Exclude secrets and unnecessary personal data. Do not publish, promote, approve, or alter shared standards.

Deliver candidate `ai/templates/knowledge-management/KNOWLEDGE_RECORD.md` records, proposed source-register updates, uncertainties, suggested owner/review trigger clearly marked as proposals, and required human review.
