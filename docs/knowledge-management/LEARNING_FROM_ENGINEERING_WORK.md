# Learning from Engineering Work

Retrospective: define event/scope/timeline/impact; collect evidence; separate facts from interpretation and hypotheses; identify contributing conditions without blame; record what worked and failed; propose actions with owners/dates; define effectiveness checks; review before promoting a lesson.

One event is not automatically a universal rule. Validate across relevant projects and versions; retain limitations and counterexamples. Do not present an untested hypothesis as root cause. Sanitize sensitive incident details. Use `ai/templates/knowledge-management/LESSON_LEARNED_RECORD.md`.
