# Operational Metrics and Review

## Balanced measures
Consider user-impacting availability, latency, error rate, incident frequency and duration, time to detect, time to restore, change failure rate, restore-test success, alert quality and capacity headroom.

## Definitions
For every metric define formula, source, unit, reporting period, owner, exclusions and known limitations. Do not fabricate baselines, targets or trends.

## Avoid metric gaming
Use balanced measures. A lower incident count may reflect under-reporting; faster closure may hide incomplete remediation. Pair quantitative indicators with qualitative review.

## Cadence
Set review frequency based on service criticality and change rate. Review incidents, risks, capacity, access, backups, SLO performance, dependencies and overdue actions.

## Actions
Assign owners and dates. Track completion and verify effectiveness. Escalate unresolved material risks to accountable owners.
