# Operational Security

## Identity and access
Use named identities, least privilege, strong authentication and time-bounded elevated access where supported. Review privileged access periodically and promptly revoke access when no longer needed.

## Secrets
Store secrets in approved secret-management mechanisms. Do not commit secrets, print them in logs, include them in tickets or expose them in AI prompts unnecessarily. Rotate credentials after suspected exposure and according to policy.

## Patching and dependencies
Track supported versions, security advisories and patch decisions. Prioritize based on exploitability, exposure and impact. Record deferred fixes with an owner, rationale and review date.

## Security monitoring
Monitor relevant authentication, authorization, administrative, network and application security events. Ensure alerts have owners and escalation paths.

## Incident coordination
Suspected data breach, credential compromise or active exploitation must follow the organization's security and privacy escalation process. Preserve evidence and avoid unauthorized investigation or disclosure.
