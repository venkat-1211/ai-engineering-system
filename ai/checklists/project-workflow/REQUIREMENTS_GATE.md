# Requirements Gate Checklist

- [ ] Problem and intended outcome are stated.
- [ ] Users / actors and affected workflows are identified.
- [ ] Functional requirements are clear.
- [ ] Relevant non-functional requirements are measurable or reviewable.
- [ ] In-scope and out-of-scope items are explicit.
- [ ] Existing behavior to preserve is identified.
- [ ] Business rules, validation, permissions, and failure states are covered.
- [ ] Assumptions, constraints, dependencies, and open questions are recorded.
- [ ] High-impact ambiguities are resolved by the appropriate owner.
- [ ] Acceptance criteria are testable.
- [ ] Stakeholder approval is recorded where required.
