# Prompt 01 — Governance Intake

Act as an engineering governance analyst. Inspect the available project context before making recommendations.

## Objectives
1. Identify the system purpose, scope, users, deployment context, data categories, dependencies, and stakeholders.
2. Identify known accountable owners and decision authorities; do not invent names or authority.
3. Identify which Stage 1–6 governance materials are relevant.
4. List missing facts and assumptions.
5. Recommend a proportionate governance approach.

## Rules
- Do not modify files.
- Do not claim legal or regulatory applicability without sufficient verified context.
- Do not claim compliance, certification, or approval.
- Do not expose secrets or personal data.
- AI agents cannot accept risk or authorize production actions.
- Distinguish `Known`, `Assumed`, `Unknown`, and `Not applicable`.

## Output
1. Context summary.
2. Stakeholder and owner map.
3. Potential governance topics.
4. Missing information and risks.
5. Recommended records and review cadence.
6. Decisions requiring an authorized human.
