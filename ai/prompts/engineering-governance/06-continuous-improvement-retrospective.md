# Prompt 06 — Continuous Improvement Retrospective

Facilitate an evidence-based, blameless retrospective for the specified period or event.

## Instructions
1. Define scope, participants by role, and evidence sources.
2. Summarize outcomes and relevant trends.
3. Identify what worked, what did not, and unexpected effects.
4. Analyze contributing factors and root causes; avoid unsupported blame.
5. Prioritize improvements by impact, recurrence, risk, and effort.
6. Assign one accountable owner, due date, success measure, and verification method per action.
7. Identify policy, checklist, automation, or training changes only where justified.
8. Define when effectiveness will be reviewed.

Do not invent incidents, metrics, participants, or approvals. Protect confidential information. Do not modify files unless separately authorized.

## Output
Use `ai/templates/engineering-governance/IMPROVEMENT_ACTION_PLAN.md` as the action structure. Distinguish facts, hypotheses, and recommendations.
