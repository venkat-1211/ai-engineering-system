## Scoring notes
Define likelihood and impact scales before scoring. Record uncertainty and rationale. Do not treat a numeric score as objective precision.

## Risk decisions
For each accepted residual risk, record the authorized human approver, authority basis, date, scope, and review date. AI agents cannot accept risk.
