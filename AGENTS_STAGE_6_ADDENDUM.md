## Suggested integration topics

- Require governance controls to be proportionate to project risk and impact.
- Identify accountable human owners for policy, risk acceptance, compliance decisions, and production approvals.
- Require evidence before reporting that a control was implemented, tested, approved, or is compliant.
- Record risk decisions, exceptions, approvals, review dates, and corrective actions.
- Do not infer regulatory applicability or claim certification or legal compliance.
- Require exceptions to have a scope, rationale, owner, compensating controls, approval, expiry, and review.
- Track quality using defined metrics with sources, measurement periods, and limitations.
- Use retrospectives and root-cause analysis to drive measurable corrective actions.

## Precedence

All existing Stage 1–5 safety, security, permission, testing, human-approval, project-workflow, and orchestration rules remain authoritative. This addendum must not weaken them.
