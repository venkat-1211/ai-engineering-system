# Prompt 04 — Knowledge Review and Refresh (Read-Only)
Review specified records against current authoritative sources. Check claims, provenance, version, scope, sensitivity, owner, freshness, duplicates, conflicts, and supersession. Do not edit, delete, archive, approve, or promote records; do not invent evidence or dates.

For each record recommend Keep active / Update required / Mark stale / Supersede candidate / Archive candidate, with evidence, proposed correction, risk, and human owner. Give overall verdict and prioritized findings.
