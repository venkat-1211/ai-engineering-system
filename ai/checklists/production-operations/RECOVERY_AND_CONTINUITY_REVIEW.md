# Recovery and Continuity Review

- [ ] Critical services, data and dependencies are identified.
- [ ] RTO/RPO are approved or explicitly recorded as unknown.
- [ ] Backup coverage, retention and protection are reviewed.
- [ ] Restore test evidence is current and successful or gaps are accepted.
- [ ] Recovery strategy and activation authority are documented.
- [ ] Exercise outcomes and corrective actions are tracked.
- [ ] Return-to-normal and fallback steps are defined.
- [ ] Claims match actual test evidence.
