# Prompt 03 — Reliability and SLO Review

Read root instructions and Stage 7 reliability guidance. Inspect available service behavior, monitoring evidence, incident history and stakeholder-approved requirements.

Propose candidate SLIs only where they represent user impact. For each, define numerator/denominator or measurement method, source, window, exclusions and limitations. Do not invent baselines, SLO targets, SLAs, error budgets or business requirements. Mark unknown values explicitly and identify the human stakeholder who must decide.

Do not modify files or access production systems. Provide an evidence-based assessment, measurement gaps and a proposed review plan.
